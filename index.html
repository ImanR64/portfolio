What I can do for you

Four services, each scoped to a fixed deliverable so you know what you get before we start.

Nanoparticle design and organelle targeting

Surface functionalization strategy, peptide and ligand conjugation, and a characterization plan that will convince a reviewer or an investor. Ten years of hands-on nanocarrier work, from synthesis to cell and tissue uptake studies.

  • Formulation and conjugation chemistry review
  • Targeting strategy for mitochondria, nucleus, ER and other compartments
  • Analytical QC plan: chromatography, spectroscopy, size and zeta
Good fit: nanomedicine startups, drug-delivery groups, CROs

Imaging strategy and data interpretation

Which technique answers your question, how to prepare the sample, and what the images actually show. Cryo-EM and cryo-ET, X-ray microscopy at synchrotron beamlines, AFM and light microscopy, plus structural-biology modelling of the result.

  • Technique selection and experiment design
  • Beamtime and cryo-EM session preparation
  • Structure-function analysis with PyMOL and ChimeraX
  • Protein expression and purification for structural work
Good fit: labs without in-house EM or synchrotron experience

Analysis pipelines and AI-assisted reporting

Reproducible image-analysis and statistics workflows in Python, R and Fiji, and AI-accelerated literature reviews, feasibility studies and technical reports that a scientist has checked line by line.

  • Image segmentation and quantification scripts you own
  • Cell-model design: iPSC lines, CRISPR knock-outs and knock-ins
  • Biostatistics: GLMs, mixed models, reporting figures
  • Literature and prior-art reviews, delivered in days
Good fit: teams drowning in microscopy data or papers

China–Europe technical liaison

I did my PhD in Beijing and have worked nine years in Swiss research. I read the science on both sides, and I can audit a CRO data package, sit in on technical calls, or translate a protocol without losing the chemistry.

  • Technical due diligence on Chinese CRO or supplier data
  • Protocol and report translation, English and Chinese
  • Partner search and first-contact support
Good fit: Swiss and EU biotechs sourcing or partnering in China
